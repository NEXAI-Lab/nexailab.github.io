Research

Exploring intelligent systems

Our research spans foundational AI methods and interdisciplinary applications. We connect ideas across language, vision, software, healthcare, and the environment.

01

Large Language Models & Generative AI

We study large-scale generative systems, their reasoning abilities, and their use in multimodal and action-oriented settings.

  • Large Language Models
  • Reasoning for LLM
  • LLM for Software Engineering
  • Vision-Language Models
  • Vision-Language-Action Models
  • Multimodal AI
  • Generative AI
02

Computer Vision & 3D Vision

We develop learning-based approaches for understanding visual scenes across images, videos, and three-dimensional data.

  • Video Object Segmentation
  • Semantic Segmentation
  • 3D Reconstruction
  • Image Understanding
  • Video Understanding
  • Deep Learning for Computer Vision

AI for Software Engineering

We investigate how AI can support software development, maintenance, and security.

  • LLM for Software Engineering
  • Code Generation
  • Code Understanding
  • Software Repository Mining
  • AI-assisted Software Engineering
  • Software Security

Smart Healthcare & Biomedical AI

We explore machine learning for health data, biomedical signals, and clinical decision support.

  • Machine Learning for Healthcare
  • Deep Learning for Healthcare
  • Biomedical Signal Analysis
  • EEG Analysis
  • COPD
  • Alzheimer's Disease
  • Clinical Decision Support
  • AI for Medical Data

Time-Series & Wearable Intelligence

  • Environmental AI
  • AI for Sustainable Development
