Join NEXAI Lab

Bring your curiosity to AI research

NEXAI Lab welcomes motivated students interested in artificial intelligence research and real-world AI applications.

Who can apply

Students at different stages

We welcome undergraduate and graduate students interested in AI research, including students preparing for MSc or PhD applications.

Helpful skills

Bring what you know

You do not need to know every skill listed here. Curiosity and a willingness to learn matter.

  • Python
  • Machine Learning
  • Deep Learning
  • PyTorch
  • Computer Vision
  • NLP
  • LLM
  • Software Engineering
  • Data Analysis

What to expect

Support for your research journey

  • Opportunity to contribute to real research projects and collaborations with researchers in Vietnam and abroad.
  • Support for research methodology, reading and writing papers, and building a research profile.
  • Work toward submissions to suitable venues, including CVPR, EMNLP, AAAI, and, where appropriate, SCIE Q1 journals.
  • Potential to contribute to international publications; publication outcomes are not guaranteed.
  • Support for MSc and PhD scholarship applications abroad and research portfolio development.
  • Explore current questions in LLMs, multimodal AI, computer vision, healthcare, and other AI applications.
